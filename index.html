Method & Sources

Transparent standards for evaluating evidence and building the chronology.

Core Principle

This reconstruction treats climatic records, anomalous archaeology, global myth patterns, and the most coherent ancient textual sequences as recoverable data. No claim of final proof is made — only of coherence: the largest number of independent data points currently clicking into place when organised around special origin → disruption → residual re-expression.

Evidence Hierarchy

  • 1 High-resolution climatic proxies (ice cores, speleothems, varves) with tight chronological control
  • 2 Radiocarbon-dated archaeological contexts with clear stratigraphy
  • 3 Independent multi-site geological or paleoenvironmental signals
  • 4 Cross-cultural mythic motifs with structural consistency and wide distribution
  • 5 Ancient textual sequences that preserve ordered chronological memory
  • 6 Interpretive synthesis (always secondary and revisable)

Evidence Categories Used

Archaeology · Paleoclimate · Geology · Genetics · Anthropology · Ancient texts · Comparative mythology · Astronomy · Historical records

Confidence Assignment

Each timeline event and major claim receives a confidence statement based on: quantity of independent lines, quality of dating, degree of consensus on the raw data, and remaining uncertainty about interpretation. High confidence means the underlying data are robust; it does not mean every interpretive link is settled.

What This Platform Does Not Claim

It does not assert that every traditional narrative is literal history. It does not dismiss mainstream archaeological frameworks. It does not treat coherence as proof. It simply organises the strongest available signals into a single chronological instrument and keeps the relative strength of each signal visible.
