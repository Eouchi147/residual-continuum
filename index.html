How to read the meters

These are transparent statements of relative confidence. High weight means data converge clearly. Moderate means alignment is coherent but alternative readings remain viable. The purpose is clarity, not persuasion.

The reconstruction claims that the largest number of independent lines currently click into place when organised around special origin → disruption → residual re-expression. Future evidence may strengthen or weaken any individual link. The meters keep relative strength visible.
