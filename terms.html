What the archive is

Every case gives a graded verdict, from Established to Ruled out. A verdict is our reading of the published evidence on the date shown; it is not a final demonstration and it can change when the evidence does. Matters of faith are never rated. Films and pages are researched and written with AI assistance, checked against the sources they list, and narrated by a synthetic voice. Drawings and reconstructions are schematic.
