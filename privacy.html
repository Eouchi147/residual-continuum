Residual Continuum

Privacy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Residual Continuum (residualcontinuum.com) is an independent archive of short films and case pages about historical mysteries. We built it to be read, not to watch its readers. This page explains, in plain words, what little data is involved.

What the site does not do

  • No accounts or sign-ups, and no advertising.
  • No analytics, tracking cookies or third-party trackers.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone.

What stays in your browser

Your light or dark theme choice, and similar display preferences, are saved in your own browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and you can clear them at any time.

The site assistant

If you ask the assistant a question, the text of your question is sent to an AI model through OpenRouter so that an answer can be written. Please do not type personal information into it. We do not keep a history of questions beyond the short-lived technical logs of our host.

The contact form

If you write to us through the contact form, your message, and a reply address if you choose to give one, are emailed to the editor through the email service Resend. We use them only to read and answer your message.

Hosting

The site runs on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes standard request data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver pages and protect the service from abuse.

Our social media accounts and the publishing tool

We publish our own films on our own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X. A private publishing tool on this site connects only to those accounts of ours, through each platform's official API, to upload our films with their captions and to read basic statistics about our own posts. It does not collect, store or process data about viewers or any other users. The access tokens for our accounts are stored encrypted and can be revoked from each platform's settings at any time.

Our use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service (www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Access can be revoked through the Google security settings page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Children

The site is a general audience archive and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and questions

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it. For any question about privacy, write to us through the contact form on the site.
